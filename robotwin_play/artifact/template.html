RoboTwin play episodes

Unscripted play on RoboTwin's two-armed robot: random tabletop scenes, and a robot that moves, stacks, pushes, turns and passes things between its hands. Every motion goes through RoboTwin's own planner and recorder, so the data has the same format and action statistics as RoboTwin's scripted expert demos. So far 16 of the 39 actions on this page succeed; failures are shown as they happened.

Where this stands

  • Three sets, 6 episodes each, from the same generator: clean (no injected errors), mixed (30% of actions get a mistake, mostly mild) and high-error (every action gets a severe, usually combined mistake). The chip on each episode says which. Injected mistakes are named on the action in the log.
  • Skills: move, put in a container, stack on a flat-topped thing, line up, hand over between the arms, push, turn around, press, shake, tap with a tool, pick two things at once, lift a container with both hands, tip a container out. Clean success is 61% over 28 actions (moves 7/8, presses 3/3, pushes 2/4, put-ins 1/3); high-error success is 21%.
  • Planner changes: RoboTwin's planner knew only the table and swept neighbours off it in transit, so the scene objects are now loaded as obstacles before every plan; put-downs descend straight onto the spot; objects that tip in the hand are set down as they are instead of being swung upright. Every plan is checked against the released data's joint range before the arm moves.
  • Still weak: thin things (pens, card decks) are grasped but often slip or pivot; taps with a tool register a hit about half the time; some scenes burn most of their attempts on unplannable options (now skipped after the first failure). Articulated objects (microwave, drawer, laptop, switch) are not ported yet.
  • Files: each episode is saved in RoboTwin's current format (data/) and in the format of its released datasets (data_released/), from the same recordings; log.jsonl carries the labels and the error type of each action.
